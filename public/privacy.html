Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

What Khoj collects

Nothing is stored on our servers. Searches live in the URL, so you can bookmark or share them, and they disappear when you close the tab. Your theme choice is saved in your browser only.

Server logs

The host may keep basic request logs such as IP address, browser type, and the time of a request. We use them only to keep the site running.

Job source links

Results link to public job pages on other sites. Those sites have their own privacy rules once you leave Khoj.

Changes

If Khoj starts collecting more data, this page will say so before the change takes effect.
